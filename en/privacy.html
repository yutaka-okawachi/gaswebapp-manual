Privacy policy

This website collects usage information as needed to improve search and content, understand usage, investigate problems and identify improper or duplicate access.

Information collected

When you use the search function, we may record the date and time, page viewed, search category and type, query and other search conditions, and information about your browser and operating system.

Google Analytics is also used to understand website traffic. It uses cookies and similar technologies to collect information such as pages viewed, usage, device and browser details, and approximate location.

This website does not request your name, address, email address or telephone number, or collect these details to identify users. Please do not enter personal information in search fields.

Purposes of use

Collected information is used only for the following purposes:

  • To understand patterns in searched terms and search methods.
  • To improve search functions and published content.
  • To investigate problems and check operational safety.
  • To compile usage statistics without identifying individuals.

Collected information is not used to identify individual users or deliver advertising.

Google Analytics

For information about data collection and processing by Google Analytics, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can limit measurement by disabling cookies in your browser settings or using the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Retention and management

Collected search history is retained on an ongoing basis to compare search trends over time and improve the website. Information is managed appropriately within the stated purposes.

Disclosure to third parties

Except where required by law, collected information is not provided to third parties for the purpose of identifying individual users. Processing by external services such as Google Analytics is subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised when website functions or services change. Important changes will be announced on this page.
