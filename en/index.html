👉 German Music Terms Dictionary

A compact dictionary of general meanings and musical translation examples for selected German terms. Its terms and sources are not exhaustive; entries are added as needed. The following composers are represented.

  • RW, GM and RS (including orchestral works by RS)
  • Anton Bruckner
  • Paul Hindemith
  • Sigfrid Karg-Elert

⚠️ The RW and RS pages primarily cover vocal and stage directions. Orchestral directions that can be understood using the GM entries are generally omitted. Italian and French terms are generally outside the scope of this database.

⚠️ Lyrics are outside the scope of this database. Please consult established translations.
